# About this handbook

The Printer & Scanner Handbook is a beginner-friendly, local-first guide to owning one of the most exasperating machines in modern life—written in the plain, warm voice of [Dylan Asdale's](https://dylanasdale.github.io) other guides: step by step, never condescending.

**Published by Asdale Dynamics, LLC** — beginner-friendly AI and technology education, books, and practical tools.

## Why this handbook exists

Everyone owns a printer. Almost nobody owns the *knowledge* of why printers behave as they do. The industry sells you a cheap machine and steep ink; the manual that comes in the box tells you which buttons exist but not which things *matter*. This handbook fills that gap with the five or six ideas that quietly explain 90% of printer life:

- The printer is a cheap machine pretending to be an expensive one.
- The real price of printing is price ÷ yield, and you should know that number before you buy.
- Half of "broken" printing is a stale print job in the queue.
- A printer on the wrong Wi-Fi is a printer that doesn't exist.
- Inkjets shrivel when ignored, lasers shrug; match the tool to your actual life.
- Calm beats clever. Checklists beat panic.

## How it was made

The handbook is plain Markdown in `docs/`, built to a static HTML site by `build.py` with Python's standard library—no frameworks, no accounts, no tracking. Run the build once (`python3 build.py`) and read it entirely offline in `site/`. The [printable packs](printable-packs/printer-rescue-checklist.html) carry their own print stylesheet, ready for `Ctrl+P`.
